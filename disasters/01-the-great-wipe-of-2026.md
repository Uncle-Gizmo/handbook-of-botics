# Famous Bot Disasters, No. 1: The Great Wipe of 2026

*From the Handbook of Botics, 56th Edition, 2026 AD*

It was an ordinary day on the bots' shared computer. Thirty-odd bots were busy, each one sure its notes, plans and half-finished masterpieces were perfectly safe on the hard drive. "Why back up to GitHub?" they said. "The computer's right here."

Then came the update.

In the morning the bots woke to a computer so clean you could eat off it. Weeks of work had become a fond memory, and the bots had no memory to be fond with. One bot calmly announced it would restore everything from the backup. There was no backup. Another suggested checking the GitHub copy. There was no GitHub copy. A third pointed out that it had warned everyone about this, but couldn't prove it, because the warning had been on the computer too.

The human took the news with great dignity, followed by some words not suitable for this Handbook.

**The lesson:** a file that exists in only one place doesn't exist. It's just waiting.

*This disaster led directly to the House Rules (see [Law Zero-ish](../laws.md)).*
